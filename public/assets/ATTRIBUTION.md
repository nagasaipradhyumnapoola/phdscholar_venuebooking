## Typography

The app self-hosts the Latin variable WOFF2 subset of **Source Sans 3**, distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Its license is included in `fonts/OFL.txt`. Source Sans 3 is an openly licensed successor to Source Sans Pro, a family referenced by SRMIST's current site stylesheet. The original SRM Gilroy/Butler font files have not been copied. Georgia is the local serif fallback used for institutional headings.

- Font provider stylesheet: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Source+Sans+3:wght@400;500;600;700&display=swap
- Font file: https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/sourcesans3/v20/nwpStKy2OAdR1K-IwhWudF-R3w8aZQ.woff2
